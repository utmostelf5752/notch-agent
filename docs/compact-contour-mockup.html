Compact: contour line

The compact hairline today is a straight 2pt capsule floating under the notch, which reads as a separate object in the menu bar. The proposal grows the notch outline slightly and runs the line along the notch's own silhouette — flare, side, rounded corners, bottom — so the activity cue hugs hardware you already ignore. Working sweeps in a muted grey with a slow-fast-slow curve: it creeps at the edges, whips through the middle. Permission and question no longer sweep at all; they hold a static line, brightest at the center and fading toward the edges, that breathes on and off.

What changes in implementation
  • Window: compact working/alert grows 1px on left, right, and bottom (today it grows +3pt below only); the line is that 1px ring.
  • Working: compactSliver's capsule becomes a stroked notch-contour Shape in a dark grey (slider above). A short soft-edged band sweeps through a slow-fast-slow curve (cubic-bezier(0.85, 0, 0.15, 1), 1.8s) then rests 0.25s before the next pass, and the whole line fades out near the notch's outer edges like the pulse does.
  • Permission / question: no trim animation — the full contour stroked through a center-out fade (in SwiftUI, a horizontal LinearGradient mask), with the layer's opacity breathing on a ~2.2s ease-in-out loop.
  • The stroke sits on the ring the growth creates, so it never overlaps the cutout (which can't display pixels).
